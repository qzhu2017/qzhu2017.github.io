Xuhui Yang was a visiting PhD student in Materials Science from Fuzhou University, China. She received her Master Degree in Materials Science and Engineering from the University of Science and Technology Beijing. She focuses on first-principle calculations of new materials, especially electronic properties. She started to work under the instruction of Qiang Zhu between 2018-2020.
