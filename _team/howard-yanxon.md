Howard Yanxon is currently a postdoc, studying condensed matter physics. He focuses on modeling and simulating new materials for sustainable energy, using machine learning and first-principle calculations. These materials will hopefully be useful for the advancement of technology. He started working in Dr. Zhu’s group in early 2018 and completed his PhD in Winter 2020.
