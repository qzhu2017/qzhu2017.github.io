Byungkyun Kang is currently a research assistant professor. He received his B.S. in Physics from Texas Tech University (TTU) in 2014. 
From 2015 to 2020, Dr Kang was a Research Associate at Unsan National Institute of Science and Technology in South Korea, Arkansas State University, and Brookhaven National Laboratory. In addition, Dr Kang was a software engineer working for a company from 2006 to 2009.
He joined the Zhu's group in January 2021. He will focus on the code development for machine learning force field development and nanoparticle simulations.
