\textbf {\large Course Outline:}
\begin{itemize} 
\item Statistical basis of thermodynamics
\item Ensemble theory
\item (Micro) canonical ensemble
\item Grand canonical ensemble
\item Quantum statistics of ideal gas and simple solid
\item Phase transitions
\item Computer simulation\\
\end{itemize}

\textbf {\large Course Description}:\\
This course covers elements of statistical physics from fundamental of classical statistic mechanics to quantum statistics. Approximately, we spend two weeks for each chapter (1-7 in Pathria's textbook). The weekly coverage might change as it depends on the progress of the class. There will be one exam during the semester, either take-home or in-class work. This final exam will cover all materials taught in this semester. You may work with others on the homework, but take-home exams must be done {\bf strictly by yourself}. Barring documentable emergencies or observance of a certifiable regious holiday, all exams must be taken at the time and place specified.
\\\\
\textbf {\large Learning Outcomes}:\\
\begin{itemize} 
\item understand the microscopic basis for thermodynamics
\item understand the macroscopic properties from micro level
\item understand the concept of equilibrium and non-equilibrium
\item understand the property of idea gas at both classical and quantum mechanic level
\item understand phase transition
\end{itemize}
