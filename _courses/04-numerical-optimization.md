# Description
Various optmization methods used in scientific computing through the [Julia language](https://julialang.org/). 10-week course, hosted through WebEx, and open to anyone interested.

## Textbooks
- [_Algorithms for Optimization_ by Mykel J. Kochenderfer and Tim A. Wheeler](https://mitpress.mit.edu/books/algorithms-optimization)

## Terms Offered
- 2020 Summer
