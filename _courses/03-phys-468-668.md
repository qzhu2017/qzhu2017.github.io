# Description
Principles and applications of statistical mechanics. Quantum statistics of ideal gas and simple solids. Transport theory, irreversible processes and fluctuations.

Credits: 3

Prerequisites: PHYS 467

## Textbooks
- [_Statistical Mechanics_, R. K. Pathria and P. D. Beale](https://www.elsevier.com/books/statistical-mechanics/beale/978-0-12-382188-1)

## Terms Offered
- 2018 Spring
- 2020 Spring
